## Not obtained

- archive.org `synthmanual-korg-ms-20-service-notes`: two transfers reset by the server; the second
  pass got it, and it is the same scan as scan A.
- **Pirkle AES e-Brief 103, "Modeling the Korg35 Lowpass and Highpass Filters" (AES 135, 2013)**: paywalled at
  aes.org (elib 16938). The two free app notes above cover the same material.
- **synth-diy list post "Korg 35 IC Q1?" (2001-01)**, https://synth-diy.org/pipermail/synth-diy/2001-January/035795.html:
  synth-diy.org serves an Anubis bot check. Not attempted, and there is no Wayback copy.
- **ModWiggler "How accurate are the basic MS20 filter modules?"** (t=286815) and **Gearspace "MS-20 MK1/MK2
  differences"**: Cloudflare challenge pages, no Wayback copies. Search snippets say they hold a user's
  claim that the MKII HPF is 6 dB/oct and that vintage units have ~20 % more resonance than the mini. Anecdotal.
- **Data sheets**: µPC14315 (NEC +15 V regulator; the later KLM-127 shows a 7815 there), 2SC1583 (Mitsubishi
  dual NPN), 2SA798 (dual PNP), 2SC1685, 2SC644, 2SK94 (NEC JFET), µPD4011C (NEC original), the GD4-203RD LED
  and the HTV-P873 photocoupler. Only aggregator HTML pages were found, with no manufacturer PDF, Wayback copy
  or plain-PDF mirror. National's own LM13600 Wayback copy returned text, not a PDF, so the TI E2E copy of
  the same sheet was used.
- **Original 5000 px GIF scans** of Korg's letter (Czech's geocities `korg2.gif`, `korgsch2b.gif`): never archived
  (410). The PDF above is a ~4000 px rendering of them.
- **Measurements of real units**: none found for a Rev1 (KORG35) unit, whether filter response, VCO scope
  traces, envelope times or noise spectra. The nearest are: Parker et al. (DAFx-19), who measured a built
  Rev2 LPF circuit; Korg's KORG35 test-circuit sketch; Tanaka's scope shots of a Korg-style PUT VCO core;
  and Stinchcombe's simulations. The prc68.com MS-20 page has only MS-20 mini CV/frequency tables and
  YouTube links. Attack Magazine's white-noise filter plot doesn't say which MS-20 was used.
