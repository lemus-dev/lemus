## 1. Noise generator (noise.cir)

### 1.1 How it works, as drawn

- **Source.** +15 V through R31 100k to a node held quiet by C5 10µ/16V (+ up), then R32 1M to
  TR3's emitter. TR3 (2SC644R; PL "2SC-644R × 1") has its base on ground and its collector lead drawn
  open. The arrowheads are not legible in any copy; the emitter must be the lead on R32: only the
  emitter-base junction breaks down below 15 V (a collector-base junction would not conduct), so the
  junction is held in reverse breakdown at a few µA (≈ (15 − V_BR)/1.1M, 7.2 µA for a 7 V
  breakdown) [I]. Its avalanche noise is the source.
- **TR2** (2SC1685): C6 1µ/50V (+ on TR3's side) couples TR3's emitter into its base. Collector on
  R34 4.7k from +15 V; R33 2.2M and C7 47p in parallel from collector to base (its bias and its
  shunt feedback); emitter to ground.
- **IC4b** (1/2 4558, pins 5, 6, 7): C8 10µ/16V (+ on the collector) and R36 10k into pin 6; pin 5
  grounded; VR3 100k (trim, marked ❶) from pin 6 to pin 7, its wiper drawn tied to the pin 7 end: a
  rheostat, gain 0 to −10. Pin 7 through **R37 1k** is check point 11: W NOISE (J25, "5VP-P") and
  "TO VCO-1 WAVE FORM" (the VCO 1 wave switch's noise position; both after R37).
- **IC4a** (pins 1, 2, 3): R35 6.8k from pin 7 into pin 2; R28 220k ∥ C2 0.022 µ from pin 2 to pin 1;
  pin 3 grounded. Pin 1 through **R30 1k** is check point 10: P NOISE (J19). One pole: gain
  −R28/R35 = −32.35 (30.2 dB) below 1/(2π · 220k · 0.022µ) = **32.9 Hz**, −6 dB/octave above.

### 1.3 The netlist and its input

`noise` is TR3's open-circuit noise voltage (volts), in series with a 7 V stand-in for the breakdown
voltage (`.param vbr`: only C6's charge depends on it) and the junction's dynamic resistance
(`.param rz3`, 1k since 2026-11-24, before 10k: `compare.md` §2). Neither is known: no 2SC644 sheet was found (semiconductors.md), and the
solver has no avalanche model. **rz3 matters**: TR2's input (R33 over the stage's gain, in parallel
with r_π) is a few kΩ, and C7 Miller-multiplied by the stage's gain (about 160) sets the top of the
white noise's band according to the source resistance. Its spectrum and level are to be fitted to
recordings, together with `trim_VR3`.

DC operating point (models.cir 2026-11-15, rz3 10k): TR3's emitter 7.072 V (7.2 µA), TR2's base
0.623 V, collector **9.751 V** (1.12 mA); ngspice 9.751 V (6 µV apart: the two programs' V_T).

- **White and pink levels.** SPEC §1 gives both outputs as "5Vp-p ±20" (±20 %, so the two within

  the netlist), so "pink" is a red (1/f²) spectrum above 33 Hz and white below, not −3 dB/octave.
  OM p. 7: "Pink noise is a 'darker' sounding white noise, with reduced high frequency components".
- Against ngspice (`noise_step`, 192 kHz): white 0.163 % of range largest, 0.007 % RMS; pink 0.059 % /
  0.010 %; |H| within 0.06 dB to 20 kHz (white 34.97 / 35.03 dB at 20 kHz). `tr2c` 1.66 % of its
  0.42 mV range is the 6.6 µV DC offset. `noise_step_rz1k`: white 0.61 %, pink 0.055 %.

## 2. Sample and hold (sh.cir)

### 2.1 How it works, as drawn

- **IN** (J2, check point 1): the jack's tip only, R10 100k to ground: **no normal** (BD and OM p. 2
  draw IN, CLOCK IN and OUT with nothing feeding them; OM p. 7 has the S/H triggered "e.g. from the MG
  rectangle wave" by a cable). R5 10k into IC2a (1/2 4558, pin 3; pin 2 tied to pin 1: follower).
- **Switch.** IC2a's output through R4 470Ω to F1 (2SK30A, rank GR: "K30A (GR)", PL "2SK-30(GR)"),
  drain and source between R4 and C1 0.1µ (to ground; check point 12 on its node). R3 1M from the R4
  node to the gate; D1 from the gate (anode) to IC2b's output (cathode). The gate arrow points into
  the channel: N-channel.
- **Clock** (J5 "CLOLK" [sic], check point 3): R38 220k from +15 V and R13 100k to ground (4.69 V
  unpatched), R8 10k into IC2b's + (pin 5); its − (pin 6) at R6 10k from +15 V / R9 1k to ground,
  1.364 V. IC2b runs open loop. Clock above 1.364 V: IC2b high (+14 V), D1 off, R3 holds the gate at
  the drain's voltage, F1 conducts: **tracking**. Clock below: IC2b at −14 V, D1 pulls the gate to
  about −13.6 V, F1 off: **holding**. SPEC: "Clock trigger input (+5V → GND)". Unpatched, it tracks.
- **Output.** IC1 (CA3140E, "FET") follows C1 (pin 3; its − input drawn tied to its output); VR1 10k B
  with R11 1k to −15 V on the offset-null pins; output through R7 1k to OUT (J8, check point 2).

### 2.3 Reading notes

- IC1's output is labelled "1" in all three copies; the CA3140's output is pin 6 (pins 1 and 5 are
  offset null, data sheet): a drafting slip.
- The clock jack's label reads "CLOLK" in all copies.
- D1's bar is at its lower end (towards IC2b) in A and T: anode on the gate. The other way round the
  switch could never turn off.

### 2.4 Checks

- **Acquisition** (`sh_track_hold`, `sh_step_down_mg`, a 10 V step while tracking; models.cir
  2SK30A_GR: I_DSS 4.11 mA, so F1 slews C1 at 41 V/ms, then (R4 + r_on 255 Ω) C1 = 72.5 µs): rising
  365 µs to 1 % (0.1 V), 531 µs to 10 mV, 698 µs to 1 mV; falling 427, 594, 766 µs. A short clock
  (an MG rectangle at its narrowest) shorter than this samples part of the step.
- **Droop**: the models have no gate leakage beyond IS 1e-14 (0.19 mV/s in the netlist, the same in
  ngspice). From the sheets: F1's I_GSS ≤ 1 nA at V_GS −30 V (Toshiba 2SK30ATM p. 1) gives ≤ 10 mV/s
  into 0.1 µF; IC1's input current 10 pA typ, 50 pA max (Renesas FN957 p. 3) gives 0.1-0.5 mV/s. C1's
  leakage is unknown (its dielectric is not listed: PL "Mylar capacitors not listed").
- **Hold step** (not modelled: no JFET capacitances): the gate falls about 18.6 V (from +5 V to
  −13.6 V); through C_gs ≈ C_iss − C_rss = 8.2 − 2.6 pF (2SK30ATM p. 1) that is at most
  5.6 p × 18.6 / 0.1µ ≈ **−1.0 mV** on C1.
- **Clock threshold** 1.364 V (R6/R9); the unpatched clock sits at 4.69 V, so an unpatched S&H tracks
  its input; IN unpatched is 0 V (R10). SPEC: "Sample signal input (5Vp-p max.)", "S/H output (5Vp-p
  max.)": the circuit passes ±13 V (IC1's swing).
- Against ngspice (192 kHz): `shout` 0.020 % of range largest (0.010 % outside the clock edges),
  0.003 % RMS; `gate` and `ic2b` 5.5 % and 3.7 % at single samples while IC2b slews through its
  28 V swing at 1 V/µs (0.012 % and 0.000 % elsewhere). `sh_step_down_mg`: `shout` 0.87 % at the
  input step's sample, 0.16 % elsewhere.

## 3. Modulation VCA (mvca.cir)

### 3.1 How it works, as drawn

- **IN** (J10, check point 4), no normal: R17 100k into IC3a (1/2 4558, pin 2), R15 75k feedback,
  pin 3 to ground through R16 47k: gain −0.75.
- **PC1**, the photocoupler (PCB "PC1"; PL "HTV-P873-G35-201B", Hamamatsu, "HTV" the old Hamamatsu TV
  name; drawn as a circled resistor with an arrow from the LED): its CdS cell from IC3a's output to
  IC3b's − input (pin 6; pin 5 grounded). IC3b's feedback: R14 2.2k in series with VR2 22k B (trim ❶,
  drawn as a rheostat) in parallel with R12 10k: R_f = 2.2k + (VR2 ∥ 10k), 2.20k to 9.08k. Gain
  −R_f / R_CdS; overall **+0.75 R_f / R_CdS**. OUT through R23 470Ω (J16, check point 5).
- **LED drive.** The LED's anode on +15 V, its cathode through R24 1k to TR1's collector (2SC1685).
  TR1's emitter: R26 1k to ground and R27 33k to −15 V (the emitter sits at −0.441 V with TR1 off, so
  it starts conducting with its base near +0.1 V). Base: R22 10k from the CONTROL node, R25 100k to
  ground, D2 from ground (anode) to the base, clamping negative controls.
- **CONTROL** (J15, check point 6) is **not a switched jack**: the drawing's normalled jacks on
  circuit diagram (1) (J4, J26) have a separate switch contact with an arrow; J15 has only its tip,
  and "FROM EG-1" reaches that tip through R20 10k (§4). OM p. 7: "The MVCA control input is
  internally patched to the EG1 output". A cable's source therefore drives the node against
  EG 1's 6.67 kΩ: EG 1 keeps a share R_src / (R_src + 6.67k) of the control (13 % from a 1 kΩ output
  resistor, the usual one on this board). The netlist connects the cable through S15 with
  `.param rsrc15` (default 1 Ω, stiff, as the convention) for the patched output's resistance (§6).

### 3.3 The photocoupler: data and the law

**Data (P873, the 201B column and curve; JPDB agrees on the limits):**

- LED: V_F 2.1 V typ at 20 mA, recommended I_F 20 mA (p. 28); I_F against V_F (Figure 4, one curve
  for the series): 1.79 V at 1 mA, 1.83 at 2, 1.905 at 5, 1.99 at 10, 2.11 V at 20 mA. Fitted:
  IS 1.076e-18, N 1.997, RS 8.87 Ω, within 3.3 mV (mvca.cir's `PC1LED`). Maximum I_F 25 mA (P873) or
  35 mA (JPDB).
- Cell: R_ON **1.0 kΩ min, 5.0 kΩ max at 20 mA** (no typical); R_OFF ≥ 10 (MΩ in JPDB) 10 s after
  I_F goes off; maximum 200 V, 50 mW.
- Figure 5, R_ON against I_F for the 201B, read off the log-log curve (±5 %, ±10 % at 15-20 mA where
  it meets the P873-24 curve; two points checked by hand: 1 mA ≈ 20 kΩ, 20 mA 1.5-1.8 kΩ):

  | I_F (mA) | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 10 | 20 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | R (kΩ) | 335 | 145 | 46 | 20.5 | 9.8 | 4.3 | 2.47 | 1.5 |

  The slope γ (−d log R / d log I) falls from 1.26 (0.2-0.5 mA) through 1.06 (1-2 mA) to 0.70
  (10-20 mA).
- Response (p. 28, the 201B at 20 mA, cell in series with a battery and 50 Ω, scope on the 50 Ω so
  it reads conductance): rise t_r (0 → 63 %) **4.0 ms typ, 10 max**; fall t_f (100 → 37 %) **1.0 ms
  typ, 5.0 max**. Figure 6 (the P873-G35-380 only, its axis printed "µs" where its own table's 8 ms
  at 50 Ω shows ms) gives the drive and load dependence: at 0.1 kΩ load, t_r 16.5 ms at 5 mA against
  9.6 at 20 mA (×1.72 for a quarter of the drive), t_f 12.3 against 8.3 ms; with larger loads t_f
  reads the time to larger resistances: for the 380 at 20 mA its resistance reaches 1.5k at 8.3 ms,
  3.0k at 21 ms and 18k at 115 ms after the LED goes off, nearly linear in time.
- No light-history ("memory") or dark-recovery curve for the P873. VTL (pp. 34, 38): on turn-off a
  CdS cell's resistance "roughly triples in a few ms and then rises about linearly with time"; a
  dark-stored cell reads lower than a light-adapted one (VTL5C3 ×1.16-1.33 from 20 mA down to 0.5 mA).
  Temperature: P873 Figure 7 (the 380): +13 % at 40 °C, −9 % at 0 °C.

**The proposed law** (C++ in the running model, setting PPC1 once per sample or every few samples:
`pc1 = R / 100 MΩ`), from the LED current I (mA) = the netlist's output `ledv` (volts across R24 1k):

1. Target R_ss(I): Figure 5's table, interpolated in log R against log I; below 0.1 mA extended with
   the first segment's slope (γ 1.21); capped at **R_dark = 10 MΩ** (the sheet's R_OFF minimum).
2. Brightening (R_ss < R): conductance first order towards 1/R_ss, τ_on = 4 ms × (20 mA / I)^0.39
   (4 ms: the 201B's t_r; the exponent from the 380's ×1.72 for ×1/4 drive) [assumed: the 380's
   drive dependence carries over].
3. Darkening (R_ss > R): R rises linearly, dR/dt = k_r = 2.55 MΩ/s, stopping at R_ss. A conductance
   falling as 1/(1 + t/τ₀) reaches 37 % at 1.70 τ₀; t_f 1 ms from 1.5 kΩ gives τ₀ = 0.59 ms and
   k_r = 1.5k / τ₀ [assumed: the rate does not depend on the drive before turn-off; the 380's curves
   suggest about ×0.5 after 5 mA instead of 20]. It reaches 10 MΩ in 3.9 s, inside the sheet's "10 s".
4. Not modelled: light history (±15-30 %, VTL), temperature, unit spread (R_ON 1-5 kΩ: VR2 trims it).

With this law and the typical cell, VR2 at 7.76 kΩ (`trim_VR2` 0.647) gives unity gain at CONTROL
+5 V (SPEC: "Control voltage input (0~+5V)", "Signal input/output (−5V~+5V)").

- **Time constants** (the law, 48 kHz): CONTROL 0 → 5 V: gain to 63 % in 7.2 ms, 90 % in 16.8 ms;
  5 → 0 V: −6 dB in 1.9 ms, −20 dB in 17 ms, −40 dB in 191 ms, −60 dB in 1.9 s, then the dark floor
  0.75 R_f / 10 MΩ = **−66 dB**. EG 1's own times (SPEC: attack and release up to 10 s) are mostly
  longer; a fast EG 1 release is stretched by the cell below −20 dB.
- **Trim range**: VR2 reaches unity at 5 V for cells with R_ON(20 mA) between 0.89 k and 3.7 kΩ
  (scaling Figure 5's curve); the sheet allows 1.0-5.0 kΩ, so a slow (high) cell would stay below unity.
- **Swing**: IC3a clips at ±14 V (input ±18.7 V); IC3b at ±14 V (`mvca_clip`).
- Against ngspice (pc1 fixed: the netlist has no law; 96 / 48 kHz): `mvca_eg1_normal` 0.000 %,
  `mvca_patched_control` `ledv` 0.001 %, `mvca_clip` 0.001 %. At pc1 = 4.93 kΩ and `trim_VR2` 0.647 the
  netlist's gain is 0.9996.

## 5. Control wheel, momentary switch, headphone amp (phones.cir), decoupling

- **Control wheel** (J28): a 10k B pot (PL "Center click-stop 10KB × 1") with one end on + and one
  on −, a centre tap drawn to ground, its wiper to J28's tip (A, T). SPEC: "Control wheel output
  (−5V ← 0V → +5V)". With the ends on ±15 V the wiper would reach ±15 V: either the wheel turns only
  a third of the track each way from the centre, or the ends see ±15 V through resistors not drawn
  here [open question]. No netlist: the machine gives J28 ±5 V × the wheel's position, behind the
  pot's Thevenin resistance (centre 0 Ω, 1.1 kΩ at a third of a half-track).
- **Momentary switch** (J31): a push switch (PL "MS-102 × 1") from J31's tip to ground (A, T). SPEC:
  "Momentary switch output (→GND)": pressing shorts the patched input to ground; the input's own
  pull-up gives the level. No netlist.
- **Headphone amp** (phones.cir, check points 7, 8, 9): "FROM SIGNAL OUT" (KLM-127: the VOLUME pot's
  wiper, J35) into IC5a (R42 4.7k, R41 27k; pins 2, 3, 1) and IC5b (R47 4.7k, R45 27k; pins 6, 5, 7),
  both inverting at **−5.74 (15.2 dB)**, through R43 220Ω to PHONES J34's tip and R46 220Ω to its
  ring. Parts read in A and T: R41 27k, R42 4.7k, R43 220Ω, R45 27k, R46 220Ω, R47 4.7k, IC5 µPC4558
  (PCB "ICb", R41-R47 around it). There is no R44 on KLM-128.
  - Its inputs load SIGNAL OUT with R42 ∥ R47 = **2.35 kΩ to virtual ground**: the VOLUME/SIGNAL OUT
    netlist must carry that load (phones.cir takes the node's voltage).
  - Check: SIGNAL OUT's "MAX 2VPP" (BD check point 19) gives 11.5 Vpp at the op-amps, **0.40 Vpp into
    8 Ω** (2.5 mW a side), 1.46 Vpp into 32 Ω. SPEC's "Head phones output ((8Ω) 120m watts 5.6)" is out
    of reach through 220 Ω (full ±14 V swing into 8 Ω is 15 mW); the 4558's output current limit is not
    modelled. Against ngspice: 0.006 % (8 Ω), 0.267 % (600 Ω, clipping).
- **Decoupling**: C3 100µ/16V from +15 V to ground, C4 100µ/16V from ground to −15 V (+ to ground), at
  the board's power pins (circled 13 +15 V, 15 GND, 14 −15 V) (A, T). Not modelled (ideal supplies).

## 7. Reading notes and doubts

- **Drafting slips**: IC1's output labelled "1" (CA3140: pin 6); "CLOLK".
