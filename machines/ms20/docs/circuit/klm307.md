# Rev 2 filters (KLM-307): VC HPF, VC LPF and the VCA daughterboard

Netlists: `core/netlists/hpf307.cir` (IC-1, Q1, IC-2 and the KLM-127 parts that feed them),
`lpf307.cir` (IC-3, Q2, IC-4 and theirs), `vca307.cir` (Q3, Q4, IC-5): drop-in alternatives to
`hpf.cir`, `lpf.cir` and `vca.cir` (the Rev 1 KORG35 filters and VCA, `filters.md`), with the same
input, output, control and probe names. The machine runs them when the plugin's bar says FILTER: REV 2
(`Machine::setFilterRev`, since 2026-11-24). Cases: `tools/spice/cases/klm307.json`. Run each as
`models.cir,<block>.cir`.

## 2. The LM13600 (IC-1, IC-3)

Pin-out (L p. 1; both halves used): 1/16 amplifier bias (A/B), 2/15 diode bias, 3/14 +input, 4/13
-input, 5/12 output, 6 V-, 7/10 buffer input, 8/9 buffer output, 11 V+. On KLM-307 the diode-bias
pins 2 and 15 are not drawn: **the linearising diodes are unused**, and the OTAs' inputs are
attenuated instead (10k into 220 ohm, Stinchcombe's k). Both buffers of each IC are used, each with
its own load to -15 V. Pins 1 and 16 are tied and fed by the expo's output transistor through R17
(R36); R18 (R37) 10M from ground adds a constant 1.39 uA (the pins sit 1.1 V above -15 V).

The model (`.model` lines in the netlists):

| Part of the chip (L p. 3) | Netlist | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Q4/Q5 pair, Q6-Q11 mirrors | B element `ota`, gm 1: out = I_abc tanh(v_d / 2V_T) | L: gm = 19.2 I_abc at 25 C (the model 19.3 at 27 C); peak output current 500 uA at I_abc 500 uA |
| Q2 (pin 1 to the mirror) | the B element's abc junction on V-, IS 1e-14 | |
| Q12, Q13 (Darlington buffer) | one NPN `QIC.._B`, h_FE 200 x 200, NF 2, IS 1.4e-13 | the two base-emitter drops as one junction (2 V_T ln(I / IS) less V_T ln(h_FE), Q12 running at I / h_FE), its base current I / 200^2. No device figures published; h_FE 200 is what the buffer's input current implies. The buffer sources only: its load resistor is all that pulls it down |
| output stage limits | diodes to 13.6 V / -13.8 V inside the B element (`vop`, `von`, `iscl`; ports of their own until 2026-11-24) | L: peak output +14.2 / -14.4 V typ (R_L infinite). Without them an OTA whose buffer has cut off integrates its cap node far past the rails (seen: -34.8 V on IC-3's pin 5 under a 50 Hz, 8 Vpp square) |

**Reduced 2026-11-24.** As first transcribed, the chip was its sheet's transistors: D1 and Q1 (the
bias mirror, pin 1 two junctions above V-), Q3 (IS 5e-16: biasing Q12 with I_abc / 20, the sheet's
"current proportional to IABC", sized from its 0.2 uA buffer input current), Q12 and Q13 as two NPNs.
That block took four times Rev 1's solver time (37 ports against 18). Without the mirror and with the
Darlington as one NPN: 27 ports, half the time, and self-oscillating within 2.2 cents and 0.2 dB of
the full model in the LPF (CUTOFF 0.3-0.95), 10 cents and 0.44 dB in the HPF (at the top), H2 2-4 dB
higher (the buffer's DC level no longer tracks I_abc). A follower as a VCVS would have done the
same but keeps the sparse solver out (its row has nothing on the diagonal). The sheet gives no
transistor data: the full model's own figures were guesses of the same order.

Not modelled: input bias currents (I_abc / 2h_FE into 220 ohm: under 0.1 mV), offsets (0.4 mV
typ: 20 mV at the buffer outputs, inside DC loops), the output resistance and capacitances (L
p. 4), mismatch between halves (L: 0.3 dB gm tracking; the model splits the tied bias current
exactly in half), the Darlington's base-emitter breakdown.

## 3. VC HPF: IC-1, Q1, IC-2 (`hpf307.cir`)

**How it works.** OTA B (pins 12-16) has no signal input: its inverting input sees its own buffered
output (pin 9) through R3 10k into R2 220 (k = 0.0215), its + input R1 220 to ground, and it
integrates into C1, whose lower end is driven by the resonance amplifier. OTA A (pins 1-5) has pin 9
at its + input (R5/R6) and its own buffered output (pin 8) at its - input (R8/R7), and integrates
into C2, whose lower end is VCF SIG IN, the mixer's output. With w_c = 19.2 I_abc k / C and k2 the
gain from the output round the PEAK network and IC-2a (S's method, §2.1 and §7):

    H(s) = (s/w_c)(1 + s/w_c) / ((s/w_c)^2 + (2 - k2) s/w_c + 1)

a band-pass plus a high-pass, 6 dB/oct below w_c; at PEAK 0 exactly s/w_c / (1 + s/w_c), a
first-order high-pass with its -3 dB point at w_c. The output (pin 8, R9 47k to -15 V) goes through
C11 33 uF to node 2, which feeds the LPF (R19) and R10 8.2k to the PEAK pot.

## 4. VC LPF: IC-3, Q2, IC-4 (`lpf307.cir`)

**How it works.** OTA B (pins 12-16) sums the input (node 2 through R19 10k) and its own buffered
output (R22 10k) at its - input over R21 220, + input on R20 220, and integrates into C4, whose lower
end the resonance amplifier drives; OTA A follows it into C5 to ground. S's equation (5) with k1 = 1:

    Vo / Vin = -1 / ((s/w_c)^2 + (2 - k2) s/w_c + 1)

unity gain, inverting, critically damped at PEAK 0 (-3 dB at 0.644 w_c), self-oscillating at k2 =
2. S Fig. 2 is this LPF (his VR1 is the panel PEAK, his R31 3.3k is R31 + VR2 at mid, his R36 8.2k;
op-amps as uA741, PNPs as 2N3906).

The exponential converters (both filters): the reference half's collector sits at the op-amp's
virtual ground, R16/R35 pulling 31.9 uA out of it, and the op-amp drives the joined emitters through
R14/R33 until the reference half carries exactly that; the output half, its base on 2.2k to ground,
then carries 31.9 uA x exp(V_node / V_T): 17.9 mV an octave at 27 C (18.4 mV at 40 C), two OTAs
sharing it. Its base current through R13/R32 2.2k lowers the top end (11 mV, 0.6 octave, at 1 mA
with h_FE 200), so the top octave hangs on the PNP's unknown h_FE (§9.3). The servo loop is C3/C6
against the emitter's dynamic resistance through R14: about 15 kHz wide at mid range (estimated),
narrower at the top (the expo's response to a fast CV step slows there; modelled, not measured
separately).

Tapers as Rev 1 (`README.md`): A = `p2.25`, B linear. Node 5's sum, all sources stiff: 2.2k // 47k
// 47k // 270k // 1M = 1.99k, so CUTOFF moves node 5 by +-0.111 V (+-6.2 octaves at 18 mV/oct, about
12 octaves end to end before the R18 floor), FCA ADJ by +-30 mV (+-1.7 octaves), EG 2/EXT at full by
42 mV per volt at the jack (2.37 octaves/V at 27 C; measured 2.1-2.2 in the middle, §9.2). The pots'
own wiper resistance (up to 25k) is in the netlist.

**PEAK's law.** The output reaches the wiper through R10/R29 8.2k: wiper/out = R_bottom / (8.2k +
R_top // 2.2k + R_bottom) = 0 at PEAK 0, 0.339 at 5, 0.464 at 8, 0.503 at 9, 0.549 at 10 (S: 1/1.82).
k2 = that times 1 + 10k / (2.2k + VR): with VR at 0 / 1.1k / 2.2k the amplifier's gain is 5.55 /
4.03 / 3.27 and k2 at PEAK 10 is 3.05 / 2.21 / 1.80. With VR above about 1.5k (share 0.7) the filter
never self-oscillates.

**Trims and the service procedure.** The service manual's only filter adjustment is 7-4 (a-009):
with the HPF (then the LPF) at PEAK 10, turn its FC ADJ until it oscillates at 500 Hz (figure 2's
settings: CUTOFF 5). No published procedure covers Rev 2; the later drawings (G, N) print no
adjustment notes. 7-4 still works on Rev 2 (FCA ADJ in the same place and role) but only once VR1/VR2
let the filter oscillate at PEAK 10, and the pitch it lands on depends on VR1/VR2 (§9.3): **set
VR1/VR2 first, then FCA ADJ.** S guesses the factory's way: "turn the resonance to maximum, and then
adjust the preset so that the filter is comfortably oscillating". The netlists' defaults:
`trim_307vr1` = `trim_307vr2` = 0.5 (1.1k, mid-travel, S's 3.3k), then 7-4 gives `trim_vr7` 0.5207,
`trim_vr8` 0.5225 (Rev 1: 0.3305, 0.3316; the machine's `Calibration` holds the Rev 1 numbers).
FCA ADJ's range at CUTOFF 5: 166 Hz to 1.43 kHz (Rev 1's FC ADJ: 137 Hz to 7 kHz).

## 7. The later main board (KLM-127 "NEW PRODUCTION", N)

N stops at MIXER OUT (IC8, check point 13): no filter, summer or VCA is drawn on it. IC9-IC12 are
not on it; IC8 is the mixer only (Rev 1's IC8b, the HPF gain stage, is gone). The rest of the filter
section is the KLM-127 parts G draws (§5). Against Rev 1 (`filters.md`), around the filters:

1. **The KORG35s and everything they needed are not drawn on N, and KLM-307 has none of them**:
   IC9, IC10, their pin-2 dividers (R103/R105, R112/R115), the filtered -15 V (R110, C25) as their
   supply, the gain stages IC8b and IC11a with R99/R117, R101/R116, C16/C31, D9/D10, D20/D21,
   C14/C32, and the PEAK feedback R106/R113 // C27/C30 with R107/R111. Whether the empty positions
   stayed on the PCB is not shown.
2. **No cutoff summer**: IC12a/b with R93/R94, R96/R95, R98/R97 are gone; the panel sources go
   straight to KLM-307's 2.2k through new values: CUTOFF 270k (was 470k), MG and EG 2 47k (were
   100k), FCA ADJ 1M (unchanged).

## 8. Interface against `hpf.cir` / `lpf.cir`

Same: inputs `mix`, `hpfext`, `totalext` / `hpf`, `lpfext`, `totalext`; outputs `hpf`, `lpf`;
probes `h5`, `hout`, `l5`, `lout`; controls `hpf_cutoff`, `hpf_peak`, `hpf_mg`, `hpf_eg2`, `lpf_...`;
trims `trim_vr7`, `trim_vr8`; `hcv`, `lcv`.

Different:

- **New trims** `trim_307vr1` (hpf307), `trim_307vr2` (lpf307): KLM-307's VR1/VR2, the share of
  2.2k in circuit (1 = 2.2k, least resonance). Defaults 0.5.
- **`trim_vr7`, `trim_vr8` calibrate to other values** (0.5207, 0.5225): a machine that sets them
  from its Rev 1 `Calibration` (0.3305, 0.3316) puts both filters 0.58 octave low (334 Hz for 500).
  `ms20service calibrate` needs the Rev 2 order (VR1/VR2, then FCA ADJ).
- **`hcv`, `lcv` are stand-ins**, not nodes: 39.92 x (V(node 5 or 9) - 13.19 mV), the KORG35 pin-1
  voltage with the same self-oscillation pitch (fitted on `lpf.cir` against `lpf307.cir`,
  2.5-12 kHz, within 0.03 V), so `Machine::pickFilterRates`' kFastCv 2.45 V / kSlowCv 2.15 V still
  mean about 6.6 / 5 kHz. Below 1 kHz the fit is off by up to 0.4 V (harmless there).
- **No `ilpf`/`ihpf` params**: no shared filtered rail; the two blocks only meet at node 2.
- **Levels**: `hpf` is at the mixer's level (Rev 1: about 1/15 of it) and `lpf` likewise, so
  `vca.cir` (Rev 1's VCA, R123 6.2k) driven from `lpf307` would be overdriven about 30x. The Rev 2
  VCA needs its own block (§6). `lpf307.cir` carries R38/R39 as its load already.
- **The probes' meaning**: `h5`/`l5` are OTA A's output (pin 5, the second capacitor's upper end),
  `hout`/`lout` the buffer outputs (pin 8) before C11/C7.
- `.param r9` (47k as printed; §10.1).
- `.model` lines for the LM13600 devices, 2SA798 and 1S1555X3 are in the netlists: they belong in
  `models.cir` once adopted.

### 9.4 Levels and distortion

The diodes sit in the resonance loop, so (S §5) they act only near the corner; the OTAs' inputs
see the signal divided by 46 and add little. What dominates at the mixer's levels is the buffers:
they only source, so each output can go only as far negative as its load resistor pulls it against
the block's loads. LPF (R28 10k against R27, R29 + PEAK and R38/R39): the output stops at -2.6 V. HPF
(R9 47k against R8, R19 and R10 + PEAK): **-0.95 to -1.1 V** (the positive side follows the input);
with R9 4.7k it is clean to +-4 V. 500 Hz sine through the open HPF (CUTOFF 0, PEAK 0): 1 Vp in,
-0.89 / +0.94 out; 2 Vp in, -0.95 / +1.61; 4 Vp in, -0.97 / +2.63 (C11 recentres the clipped wave).
LPF open (CUTOFF 10): clean to 2 Vp, 3 Vp gives -2.58 / +3.01.

## 10. Drawing doubts

1. **R9 47K** (HPF buffer A's load, G at 4x: "R9 47K", unambiguous). The other three buffer loads
   are 4.7K (R4) and 10K (R23, R28). With 47k the HPF's output can sink only 0.32 mA, and with R8,
   R19 and the PEAK chain on it the output stops at -1.0 to -1.1 V: every signal over about 2 Vpp
   through an open HPF is half-clipped (47 % THD at 8 Vpp, §9.4), audibly unlike Rev 1 and unlike the
   usual account of Rev 2 as the tamer filter. 4.7k (R4's value, a dropped decimal point) would make
   it clean to +-4 V. The board photos (P) are too coarse for the colour bands. The netlist keeps the
   printed 47k as `.param r9`; `--param r9=4.7k` gives the alternative (case
   `hpf307_sine_drive_r9_4k7`). A sharper photo of a KLM-307 board's R9, or a Rev 2 recording of a
   loud saw through the open filters (half-wave clipped or not), would settle it.
2. **HPF FCA ADJ drawn from "+" to "+"** (G at 3.5x: both boxes "+"); the LPF's is + to -, as Rev 1's
   VR7/VR8. Taken as + to -.
3. **HPF MG/T.EXT pot printed "100 KB"**, the LPF's "100 KA" (G at 3.5x). The panel pots are Rev 1's
   (VR18, VR23: both 100 kA); taken as 100 kA.
4. **R36 printed "3.2K"** (G at 5x), a value outside E24; S's Fig. 2 has 8.2k (as the HPF's R17).
